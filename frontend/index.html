    ⚡ Rob Booker Knoxville Divergence Rules

    Knoxville Divergence detects exhaustion points where price makes a new high/low, but Momentum oscillator diverges.

    • Buy Signal (Day 1): Bullish Knoxville divergence line triggers on daily chart.
    • Buy Confirmation (Day 2): Must be a Green Candle (Close[Day 2] > Open[Day 2]) that opens and closes higher than Day 1 (Close[Day 2] > Close[Day 1]). Stop Loss is locked at Low[Day 2].
    • Buy Entry (Day 3+ Crossing): Whenever price crosses above Day 2's High (High ≥ High[Day 2]), BUY the stock immediately. (Invalidated if price drops below Day 2 Stop Loss before crossing).
    • Sell Signal (Day 1): Bearish Knoxville divergence line triggers on daily chart.
    • Sell Confirmation (Day 2): Must be a Red Candle (Close[Day 2] < Open[Day 2]) that opens and closes lower than Day 1 (Close[Day 2] < Close[Day 1]). Stop Loss is locked at High[Day 2].
    • Sell Entry (Day 3+ Crossing): Whenever price crosses below Day 2's Low (Low ≤ Low[Day 2]), SELL the stock immediately. (Invalidated if price rises above Day 2 Stop Loss before crossing).

    🟢 Dual RSI Extreme Rules

    • Buy Signal: Both 14-period RSI and 14-period RSI-SMA drop below 30.0 (Extreme Oversold).
    • Sell Signal: Both 14-period RSI and 14-period RSI-SMA rise above 70.0 (Extreme Overbought).

    📈 200 SMA Touch / Crossover Rules

    • Bullish Crossover: Daily price crosses from below the 200-day Simple Moving Average to close above it.
    • Bearish Crossover: Daily price crosses from above the 200-day SMA to close below it.

    🎯 Exit & Risk Controls

    • Target Exit: Position automatically closes when price reaches the configured Target % gain.
    • Stop Loss Exit: Position automatically closes when price touches the Stop Loss threshold (or predetermined signal candle Low/High for Knoxville). No premature selling in minor loss.
    • Time Exit (12–15 Days Profitable): If held for 12–15 trading sessions and the trade is in positive profit (PnL > 0%), exit to lock in profits.
