    ⚡ Rob Booker Knoxville Divergence Rules

    Knoxville Divergence detects exhaustion points where price makes a new high/low, but Momentum oscillator diverges.

    • Buy Signal: Bullish Knoxville divergence line triggers on daily chart.
    • Buy Confirmation: The next-day candle must be a Green Candle (Close[t+1] > Open[t+1]) and close higher than the signal day closing price (Close[t+1] > Close[t]). Entry opens upon confirmation!
    • Predetermined Stop Loss (Long): Stop Loss is locked at the lowest price of the signal day when Knoxville was formed (Low[t]).
    • Sell Signal: Bearish Knoxville divergence line triggers on daily chart.
    • Sell Confirmation: The next-day candle must be a Red Candle (Close[t+1] < Open[t+1]) and close lower than the signal day closing price (Close[t+1] < Close[t]). Entry opens upon confirmation!
    • Predetermined Stop Loss (Short): Stop Loss is locked at the highest price of the signal day when Knoxville was formed (High[t]).

    🟢 Dual RSI Extreme Rules

    • Buy Signal: Both 14-period RSI and 14-period RSI-SMA drop below 30.0 (Extreme Oversold).
    • Sell Signal: Both 14-period RSI and 14-period RSI-SMA rise above 70.0 (Extreme Overbought).

    📈 200 SMA Touch / Crossover Rules

    • Bullish Crossover: Daily price crosses from below the 200-day Simple Moving Average to close above it.
    • Bearish Crossover: Daily price crosses from above the 200-day SMA to close below it.

    🎯 Exit & Risk Controls

    • Target Exit: Position automatically closes when price reaches the configured Target % gain.
    • Stop Loss Exit: Position automatically closes when price touches the Stop Loss threshold (or predetermined signal candle Low/High for Knoxville).
    • Strict Resolution: Positions run continuously until either Target or Stop Loss is reached (no arbitrary time cutoffs).
